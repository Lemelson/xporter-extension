How XPorter works

XPorter uses your signed-in X account to read data. It does not publish posts, like posts, follow accounts, or change account settings. Requests include pauses, but X may still limit requests or temporarily restrict your account.

Export files are created in your browser. Their contents are not sent to the developer, and you do not need a separate XPorter account. XPorter also keeps usage counters locally. When the uninstall feedback page opens, it sends usage statistics separately from your exported data.

XPorter uses the X account you are already signed in to in this browser. It can export data available to that account, including your own bookmarks. You do not need to enter your X password in XPorter.

Export posts, your bookmarks, followers, following, and verified followers. Post files can include views, likes, reposts, replies, quotes, bookmark counts, dates, links, media information, and hashtags, when X provides them.

Choose CSV for spreadsheets, XLSX for Excel with optional photo previews, JSON for structured data, or TXT for reading and working with AI tools. Files are created in your browser.

Export posts from a specific time window. Date-range exports open an X search tab and scroll it automatically to gather matching posts — just keep that tab open until it finishes.

XPorter saves progress during an export. Closing the popup does not stop collection. After stopping an export or restarting the browser, use Resume while its saved data is still available. Request-limit pauses are retried automatically; if retries are exhausted, you can try Resume later.
